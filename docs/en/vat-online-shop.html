How to Calculate VAT in Your Online Shop (19% / 7%)

Guide · approx. 3 min read

The price your customers see is the gross price — including VAT. To keep your margin right, calculate net and add the tax only at the end.

Net, gross and VAT

The net price is your calculated price without tax. The gross price is the final price with tax. The formula: net × (1 + tax rate) = gross. Example: €19.48 × 1.19 = €23.20 (at 19%).

Standard or reduced rate?

In Germany the standard rate of 19% usually applies. For certain goods — such as many foods or books — the reduced rate of 7% applies. The correct rate depends on the product and should be set per item.

Common mistake: calculating on gross

Calculating directly with gross prices mixes margin and tax. Always calculate in net steps and add VAT last — so your margin stays clean and comparable regardless of the rate.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert net to gross?
Gross = net × (1 + tax rate). At 19%: net × 1.19. At 7%: net × 1.07.
When does the reduced 7% rate apply?
For certain product groups such as many foods, books or magazines. Check the classification per product.
Is this tax advice?
No. This guide and the app do not replace professional tax advice — when in doubt, ask your tax advisor.
