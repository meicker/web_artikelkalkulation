🔒 App Privacy Policy

PriceCalc Pro – Shopify App · Last updated: 31.10.2026

This privacy policy concerns the Shopify app PriceCalc Pro. For visits to this website, the website privacy policy applies.

This English version is a translation provided for convenience.

This policy describes how personal data are processed when a merchant installs and uses the app PriceCalc Pro in its Shopify shop. Visits to our website are governed by the privacy policy published there. Matters that apply only to PriceCalc Pro are set out in the App Module (Part B) at the end of this policy.

We have not appointed a data protection officer because the statutory requirements for doing so are not met (Art. 37 GDPR, § 38 BDSG (German Federal Data Protection Act)).

2. Roles

2.1 Data of the merchant. Where we process data about the merchant and its shop for the contract concerning the App, we do so as controller. This includes the data that Shopify transmits to us upon installation (Section 3), subscription and usage data, and communication with the merchant.

2.2 Data of the merchant’s customers. If an app processes personal data of the merchant’s customers (e.g. from orders), we do so on behalf of the merchant as processor under Art. 28 GDPR. The merchant is then the controller; the basis is a contract for processing on behalf between the merchant and us. Whether PriceCalc Pro processes such data is stated in the App Module.

2.3 Shopify. Shopify processes the shop’s data as an independent controller in accordance with its own privacy provisions (shopify.com/legal/privacy). The App loads program libraries from Shopify (cdn.shopify.com); in doing so, Shopify receives the IP address of the user, with whom a connection already exists within the Shopify admin.

3. Data we receive from Shopify

3.1 Upon installation, Shopify transmits master data of the shop to us. Of these, we store: shop ID, myshopify domain and shop domain, shop name, name of the shop owner, email address of the shop, country, the Shopify plan and the date the shop was created. We keep them up to date via the Shopify notification “shop/update”. Name and email address may relate to a natural person, for example in the case of sole traders; we therefore treat these data as personal data. Which of these fields PriceCalc Pro actually stores is stated in the App Module.

3.2 For a paid plan, we store the plan, identifier and term of the subscription and the start of a free trial. We do not receive payment data; payment is processed solely by Shopify.

3.3 The App accesses further data in the shop only with the access permissions that the merchant grants upon installation. Which permissions these are and what the App uses them for is stated in the App Module.

3.4 Obligation to provide data. Without the data under Section 3.1 and the granted access permissions, the App cannot be installed or used. There is no statutory or contractual obligation to provide these data to us (Art. 13(2)(e) GDPR).

5.3 Access logs. Each time the App, its interfaces or its webhooks are called, Gadget automatically logs the time, the address called, the status code, the response time and a technical identifier of the request and of the session. The purposes are security, troubleshooting and protection against misuse (Section 4, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Gadget retains these logs for 30 days and then deletes them. For the connection itself, Gadget and its service providers (Section 5.2) also process the IP address and the information sent by the browser (e.g. browser type and operating system). Gadget does not publish a fixed retention period for this information: Gadget and its service providers store access logs only for as long as necessary for security and troubleshooting; we ourselves do not store IP addresses.

5.4 Transfers to third countries. An adequacy decision of the EU Commission exists for Canada (Art. 45 GDPR). Transfers to the USA in the context of hosting are based on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR).

6. Email

6.1 App notifications. Emails that an app sends automatically, such as notifications to us or to the merchant, are sent via Gadget’s email service (Section 5). Which emails PriceCalc Pro sends is stated in the App Module.

6.2 Resend. If an app sends emails via the Resend service (Plus Five Five, Inc., San Francisco, USA), Resend processes the recipient address, content and delivery status as our processor. The basis is Resend’s contract for processing on behalf (Data Processing Addendum) with the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR); in addition, Resend is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR). Whether PriceCalc Pro uses Resend is stated in the App Module.

6.3 Support. If the merchant writes to us at support@jrmedia.software, we process its message, its address and the information it provides in order to answer the request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, for general enquiries point (f)). The mailbox is located on a mail server operated for us by Host Europe GmbH, Cologne.

7. Privacy requests from Shopify and deletion

7.1 Shopify forwards three types of requests to us that every app must handle: requests for access concerning the merchant’s customers (“customers/data_request”), deletion requests concerning the merchant’s customers (“customers/redact”) and the deletion request for the shop (“shop/redact”).

7.2 If the merchant uninstalls the App, Shopify sends us the deletion request “shop/redact” 48 hours after uninstallation. Upon its receipt, we automatically delete the data stored for the shop. The only exceptions are what the App Module expressly names as a residual record and the proof under Section 8.

7.3 Data that the App has written to the merchant’s shop are held by Shopify, not by us. They remain in the shop until the merchant deletes them there.

8. Proof of agreement to the Terms of Service

We store which version of the Terms of Service a shop agreed to and when: shop ID, myshopify domain, version, checksum of the displayed text, wording of the confirmation, app version and time. The proof is retained beyond uninstallation because it serves as proof of the conclusion of the contract and for the defence against claims (Art. 6(1)(f), Art. 17(3)(e) GDPR). We delete it as soon as claims under the contract are time-barred, as a rule three years after the end of the year in which the contract ends. Deletion takes place automatically.

9. Storage in the browser

The App stores settings and work states in the browser’s local storage (localStorage, sessionStorage). Shopify and Gadget set session cookies to authenticate the embedded app session. This access to the end device is strictly necessary for the service expressly requested by the merchant and is therefore permitted without consent (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG (German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act)). Our apps do not use analytics, tracking or advertising cookies. What PriceCalc Pro stores in the browser is stated in the App Module.

10. Emails about other apps (only with consent)

10.1 If the merchant has given consent when setting up the App or later in the settings, we occasionally send information about other Shopify apps from JRMedia and requests for a review to the shop’s email address. The legal basis is consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 7(2) no. 2 UWG). Consent is voluntary; the App can be used without restriction without it. Emails about the App itself, for example concerning billing or changed Terms of Service, are not advertising and do not depend on consent.

10.2 Consent may be withdrawn at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR): via the switch in the App’s settings, via the unsubscribe link in every such email or by email to support@jrmedia.software. Sending also ends upon uninstallation of the App.

10.3 We do not evaluate opens and clicks of these emails.

10.4 As proof, we store the granting and withdrawal together with their wording and time. We are obliged to be able to demonstrate consent (Art. 7(1) in conjunction with Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR). This proof is retained and deleted in the same way as the proof under Section 8.

11. Accounting

Shopify pays us the fees for the apps with payout statements that contain, for each shop, the shop name or domain, the amount and the period. We process these documents for our accounting and retain them for as long as required by commercial and tax law (§ 257 HGB (German Commercial Code), § 147 AO (German Fiscal Code); eight or ten years depending on the type of document). For this purpose we use the invoicing software easybill (easybill GmbH, Germany) and transmit the documents to our tax adviser, who processes them using the software and data centre of DATEV eG (Germany).

12. No automated decision-making

We do not make decisions that have legal effects on the merchant or similarly significantly affect it based solely on automated processing (Art. 22 GDPR). We always decide on a block after examining the individual case. Technical protection mechanisms of an app that merely compare a counter with a fixed threshold are described in the App Module.

13. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18) and data portability (Art. 20). To exercise these rights, write to support@jrmedia.software.

Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR). Where we process data on the basis of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest), you may object to this processing at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation. We will then no longer process the data unless we demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, or the processing serves the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims. You may withdraw consent, for example to emails about other apps (Section 10), at any time without giving reasons with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR). Objection and withdrawal require no particular form; the simplest way is by email to support@jrmedia.software.

Where data of a merchant’s customers are concerned that we process as processor (Section 2.2), data subjects should contact the merchant; we support the merchant in this.

14. Right to lodge a complaint

You may lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the Member State of your habitual residence, place of work or place of the alleged infringement (Art. 77 GDPR). The authority competent for us is the Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen (State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of North Rhine-Westphalia), Kavalleriestraße 2–4, 40213 Düsseldorf.

15. Changes

We adapt this policy when the App, the services used or the legal situation change. The current version is available in the App; the date at the top states its version date.

App Module PriceCalc Pro – Part B

B1. Access permissions and role

Upon installation, PriceCalc Pro requests the following Shopify access permissions: read and write products (read_products, write_products), read and write inventory (read_inventory, write_inventory) and read the shop’s languages (read_locales). The App cannot access orders, customers, addresses or any other data of the merchant’s customers. The Provider therefore processes only data of the merchant, as controller (Section 2.1); Section 2.2 does not apply to PriceCalc Pro.

B2. Product and price data

The App reads the shop ID and currency, product and variant data (product name, variant, SKU, purchase price, selling price), collections, vendors and tags, metafields on products and variants in the namespace “custom” (calculation values such as calculation factor, procurement costs, overhead costs, profit mark-up, buffer, VAT key and calculation method) and metafields on the shop in the namespace “artikelkalkulation” (language, initial setup, factor templates, supplier profiles with EAN prefixes, number patterns, work sessions). It writes changed prices and metafields back to the shop. The Provider does not store these data on its servers; they are processed at runtime and held in the browser. The product IDs under B4 are excepted. As a rule, these are business data of the merchant without any personal reference.

B3. Stored data relating to the shop

(1) Of the master data under Section 3.1, the App stores all fields named there, plus settings of the shop without personal reference, such as currency, time zone, primary language and tax settings. It does not store the shop’s address, telephone number or geodata.

(2) Usage and administrative data: number of requests in the current billing period, number of notifications received from Shopify and of allowance overruns, time of last activity, an administrative status (active, flagged or blocked) with reason and time, and messages that the Provider displays to the shop in the App. These data can only be viewed in an internal administration area that is technically restricted to designated administrator shops. The purpose is compliance with plan limits and the prevention of misuse (Section 4, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The request counters are reset monthly.

B4. Item allowance

To manage the item allowance (Part A, A4), the Provider stores for each shop the Shopify product IDs of the unlocked items together with the time and route of unlocking (item calculation, price list import or transfer of existing items), but neither product names nor prices; in addition, the number of occupied slots, any grandfathering and key figures for determining the allowances (number of items and variants in the shop, number of items edited with the App). In order to free the slots of deleted items, the App queries Shopify as to whether these IDs still exist. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

B5. Loading times

Shopify measures in the browser how quickly the pages of the App load (metrics LCP, CLS and INP) and passes them to the App. The Provider stores them with the page path and time in order to monitor Shopify’s performance requirements for apps (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). It does not store device, browser, IP address or country in this context. The measurements are deleted after 35 days.

B6. Feedback

If the user voluntarily answers a feedback question in the App, for example about the reason for a cancellation, the Provider stores the rating, the selected reason, optional free text, plan, shop identifier, myshopify domain and time (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, interest in improving the App). The App can be used unchanged without an answer; there is no benefit for answering. The App asks feedback questions only in the App, not by email. If an answer is received, the Provider receives an email notification with the shop domain, plan and content of the answer; it is sent via Gadget’s email service (Section 6.1). For handling, the Provider may transfer the content of an answer in anonymised form, without the shop address and without any information that would identify a shop or a person, to its task management system GitHub (GitHub, Inc., San Francisco, USA). The transfer to the USA is based on the adequacy decision for the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR), under which GitHub is certified.

B7. Email

PriceCalc Pro sends emails only via Gadget’s email service (Section 6.1), namely the notification to the Provider under B6. The App does not use Resend.

B8. Backups and storage in the browser

(1) The App downloads backup files (JSON) directly to the user’s device; they are not stored on the Provider’s servers.

(2) The App stores settings (including automatic saving, price rounding, language, backup preferences) and work states in the browser’s local storage. When reading in a supplier price list, it additionally stores there the mapping in progress (file name, supplier item numbers, decisions made); this is removed automatically after 30 days and can be deleted at any time via “Discard”. These data do not leave the browser (Section 9).

B9. Deletion and residual record

(1) Upon receipt of the deletion request “shop/redact” (Section 7.2), the Provider deletes the master data (B3 para. 1), the subscription data, the usage and administrative data (B3 para. 2), the loading times (B5) and the information on the item allowance including the product IDs (B4). For feedback answers (B6), it deletes the shop reference and free text; rating, reason and time remain as an anonymous metric without reference to a shop.

(2) A technical record remains with the shop ID and myshopify domain, the time at which the shop started its free trial and, where applicable, an existing block with time and reason. These details serve solely to prevent misuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): without them, the free trial and a block could be circumvented by reinstalling. In addition, the proof under Section 8 and the time of uninstallation, which determines its deletion period, remain. The Provider deletes the free trial, block and time of uninstallation automatically together with the proof, three years after the end of the year in which the App was uninstalled: it does not need them for longer, because claims under the contract and arising from a block are then time-barred and a shop that has not been installed for that long no longer uses the free trial repeatedly in a targeted manner. Thereafter, the platform (Section 5) retains only the shop ID and myshopify domain as the shop’s identifier.

(3) The Provider answers requests concerning the merchant’s customers (“customers/data_request”, “customers/redact”) with the information that the App does not store any data of the merchant’s customers.

(4) Otherwise: entries of deleted items in the item allowance are removed at the next reconciliation. Gadget deletes access logs after 30 days (Section 5.3). Invoices and payment receipts are created and retained by Shopify.

B10. Protection of operations

The temporary suspension of write operations under Part A, A5 merely compares a counter with a fixed threshold and does not evaluate any personal aspects; it is not an automated decision within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR (Section 12).
