4.3 Item allowance
(1) Each plan includes a maximum number of items (products) that can be calculated with the app. An item takes a slot when it is first saved in the item calculation (including by autosave) or newly created during a price-list import with a calculation from a vendor template. An item that takes a slot is called unlocked. An unlocked item can be recalculated without any limit in time or number. Variants of an item do not take a slot of their own. The item allowance is not reset.

(2) The following in particular do not take a slot: maintaining purchase prices, adding tags and variants, restructuring items, and creating items without a calculation during a price-list import – in each case within the functions of the plan booked. On plans with a limited item allowance, restoring a backup restores selling prices and calculation values only for unlocked items that are not resting; purchase prices are restored for all items. When adding or restructuring variants, the app may assign the new variants a selling price from a vendor template without this taking a slot.

(3) An item's slot becomes free when the item is deleted in the store. The app usually reconciles this automatically once the item allowance is exhausted; the user can trigger the reconciliation at any time via “Check allowance”. Archiving or setting an item to draft does not free a slot. Exchanging unlocked items for others is not possible.

(4) If the user considers the count to be incorrect, they can contact support; the Provider will check it and correct it without undue delay.

Purchase prices remain maintainable for all items; if the user changes the purchase price of a resting item, the app does not adjust its selling price (see also section 6).

(4) Before a plan change, before removing an add-on pack and before cancelling the paid plan in the app, the app shows how many items would rest afterwards.

(2) If this number exceeds the plan's allowance, the user keeps it as their item allowance (grandfathering). Add-on packs increase the protected number accordingly.

(3) If the user changes to a larger plan, grandfathering continues; the higher of the two numbers applies – the protected number or the new plan's allowance –, each plus booked add-on packs. Grandfathering ends with a plan change to a smaller plan and with the end of the paid plan (return to the Free plan). Section 4.5 then applies. Grandfathering is granted once and does not revive, not even on a later change back. The end of a grant or of a trial period (4.8) is not a plan change within the meaning of this paragraph.

Add-on packs are billed together with the plan through Shopify under section 4.2 and can be removed in the app at any time. Under Shopify's current practice: with monthly billing, an added pack is charged pro rata for the current period and a removed one is credited pro rata, the credit being offset against the next Shopify invoice; with annual billing, removal takes effect at the end of the paid year and no credit is issued. Add-on packs remain when changing between Basic and Premium. They lapse on a change to the Free or Unlimited plans and with the end of the paid plan. If add-on packs lapse, section 4.5 applies.
