App Module PriceCalc Pro – Part A

A1. Service Description

PriceCalc Pro supports the Customer in calculating selling prices in its Shopify shop, in particular through

  • the maintenance of purchase prices and calculation factors,
  • the calculation of selling prices, margins, trade margins and VAT amounts,
  • the import of purchase prices from the supplier’s price lists,
  • writing the results to product fields and metafields of the shop,
  • backing up and restoring the price data as a JSON file.

The user manual is available in the App under “User Manual”. The App shows which functions a plan includes in the “Subscription” section before booking.

A2. Limits of the service and review before saving

(1) The App calculates exclusively with the values that the Customer enters or has stored in its shop, such as purchase prices, calculation factors, percentages and VAT rates. Incorrect or incomplete values lead to incorrect results. Section 14 of the Terms of Service applies: the calculated VAT amounts, margins and selling prices do not constitute tax or legal information.

(2) When the Customer saves, the App writes prices and metafields directly and permanently to its shop. It cannot undo saved changes by itself; an earlier state can only be restored from a backup made beforehand. The Customer therefore checks the calculated prices before saving them and, before saving many items at once, makes a backup in the “Backup” section (Section 9 of the Terms of Service).

(3) The App depends on Shopify’s Admin API. Outages, changes or limitations of this interface may impair the functioning of the App (Sections 7.3 and 11 of the Terms of Service).

A3. Plans, billing and cancellation

(1) The App is offered in the plans “Free”, “Basic”, “Premium” and “Unlimited”. They differ in their range of functions and in the number of items that can be calculated with the App (item allowance). The “Free” plan is free of charge. The App shows the prices, range of functions and allowance of the plans before booking.

(2) Shopify bills paid plans either every 30 days or annually, at the Customer’s choice (Section 5 of the Terms of Service). For the paid plans, the App offers a free trial; the App shows its duration before booking (Section 4 of the Terms of Service).

(3) The cancellation function under Section 6.2 of the Terms of Service is the “Cancel subscription” button in the “Subscription” section. After the end of the paid period, the Customer continues to use the App on the “Free” plan.

A4. Item allowance

A4.1 Allocation. (1) Each plan includes a maximum number of items (products) that can be calculated with the App. An item occupies a slot when it is saved for the first time in the item calculation (including by automatic saving) or is newly created during the price list import with a calculation based on a manufacturer template. An item that occupies a slot is referred to as unlocked. An unlocked item can be recalculated without any limit in time or number. Variants of an item do not occupy a slot of their own. The item allowance is not reset.

(2) In particular, the following do not occupy a slot: maintaining purchase prices, adding tags and variants, restructuring items and creating items without a calculation during the price list import – in each case within the scope of the functions of the booked plan. In plans with a limited item allowance, restoring a backup restores selling prices and calculation values only for unlocked items that are not dormant; purchase prices are restored for all items. When adding or restructuring variants, the App may assign a selling price based on a manufacturer template to the new variants without this occupying a slot.

(3) An item’s slot becomes free when the item is deleted in the shop. As a rule, the App reconciles this automatically as soon as the item allowance is exhausted; the Customer can trigger the reconciliation at any time via “Check allowance”. Archiving or unpublishing an item does not free a slot. Unlocked items cannot be exchanged for other items.

(4) If the Customer considers the count to be incorrect, it may contact support; the Provider checks the count and corrects it without undue delay.

A4.2 Change of plan and dormant items. (1) A change of plan within the meaning of this Section is a change of the subscribed plan (Free, Basic, Premium, Unlimited). It takes effect as soon as Shopify activates the new subscription; the App applies it at the latest the next time it is opened. A change of the billing interval within the same plan and the booking or cancelling of add-on packs are not a change of plan.

(2) If the item allowance decreases – through a change to a smaller plan, through the end of the paid plan (return to the Free plan upon expiry of the paid period), through the cancellation of add-on packs or through the end of the grandfathering under A4.3 –, the items unlocked first remain editable up to the new maximum number. The time of unlocking is decisive. Items that are deemed unlocked under A4.3 (1) take precedence over all items unlocked later; among themselves and in the case of the same time, the Shopify product ID in ascending order decides. The App shows which items are active and which are dormant.

(3) The remaining items are dormant: they remain unchanged in the Customer’s shop, including prices and calculation values, but cannot be recalculated in the App until a slot becomes free again. When a slot becomes free, the dormant item unlocked earliest becomes editable again first. A larger plan or add-on packs make dormant items editable again without further action. Purchase prices remain maintainable for all items; if the Customer changes the purchase price of a dormant item, the App does not adjust its selling price.

(4) Before a change of plan, before the cancellation of an add-on pack and before a cancellation of the paid plan in the App, the App shows how many items would then be dormant.

A4.3 Transfer of existing items and grandfathering. (1) Items that were calculated with the App before 16 November 2026 are deemed unlocked from that day (transfer of existing items). The number determined by the App at the transfer and displayed in the subscription section is decisive; A4.1 (4) applies.

(2) If this number exceeds the allowance of the plan, the Customer retains it as its item allowance (grandfathering). Add-on packs increase the protected number accordingly.

(3) If the Customer changes to a larger plan, the grandfathering continues; the higher of the two numbers applies – the protected number or the allowance of the new plan –, in each case plus any booked add-on packs. The grandfathering ends with a change to a smaller plan and with the end of the paid plan (return to the Free plan). Thereafter, A4.2 applies. The grandfathering is granted once and does not revive, not even upon a later change back. The end of a grant or of a free trial (A4.5) is not a change of plan within the meaning of this paragraph.

A4.4 Add-on packs. In the Basic and Premium plans, the item allowance can be extended by add-on packs. The App shows the size, price and maximum number of packs before booking. Add-on packs are billed via Shopify together with the plan in accordance with Section 5.2 of the Terms of Service and can be cancelled in the App at any time. Under Shopify’s current practice, the following applies: with monthly billing, an added pack is charged pro rata for the current period, a cancelled pack is credited pro rata and the credit is offset against the next Shopify invoice; with annual billing, a cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid year, and no credit is given. When changing between Basic and Premium, add-on packs remain in place. When changing to the Free or Unlimited plans and upon the end of the paid plan, they lapse. If add-on packs lapse, A4.2 applies.

A4.5 Grants and free trials. If the Provider grants a plan or an allowance for a limited time, or if a free trial ends, A4.2 applies after its end. Grandfathering is neither shortened nor ended by a grant or a free trial.

A5. Protection of operations

(1) If a shop reaches a multiple of the usual number of requests, the App temporarily suspends write operations for that shop. Read access remains available. The measure ends with the next billing period and is lifted without undue delay upon request to support@jrmedia.software. It is not a block under Section 10.3 of the Terms of Service.

(2) An automatic flagging of conspicuous shops is merely an internal review signal. The Provider decides on a block in accordance with Section 10.3 of the Terms of Service.

A6. Data at the end of the contract

Prices and calculation values written by the App are stored as product fields and metafields in the Customer’s shop and remain there (Section 18.1 of the Terms of Service). In addition, the Customer can at any time download purchase and selling prices and the calculation values as a JSON file in the “Backup” section, and number patterns, supplier profiles and calculation templates separately under “Back up settings”.

A7. Processing on behalf

PriceCalc Pro does not process any personal data of the Customer’s customers; the App does not request the Shopify access permissions required for this. A contract for processing on behalf (Art. 28 GDPR) under Section 17 of the Terms of Service is therefore not required. If the Customer needs one for its own documentation, the Provider provides a template at pricecalcpro.de/avv.html.
