**App introduction** (max ~100 chars)


**App details**
```
PriceCalc Pro brings structure to your prices — right inside Shopify Admin.

Manage the purchase prices (unit cost) of all your variants and calculate
traceable sell prices from them: either with a simple calculation factor or with
the 5-step calculation using freight, handling, profit margin, buffer and VAT —
step by step up to the gross price.

Because price changes are written permanently to Shopify, safety is central:
an automatic backup before saving and a restore that shows you every single
change for review before it is written back.

Ideal for merchants with many variants — with filters by collection, vendor, tag
and SKU, batch saving, Excel import/export and optional autosave.

• Manage cost prices clearly — one by one or in a batch
• Sell price by factor or 5-step calculation
• VAT (standard & reduced rate) per product
• Price rounding to clean end amounts
• Full data backup as JSON (export & restore with diff)
• Excel export & import for offline editing
• Autosave and work sessions for large catalogs
• German & English

Free plan with 20 actions/month. No end-customer data. Cancel anytime via Shopify.
```

**Key benefits** (3× title + text)
```
1) From cost to the right price
   Calculate sell prices traceably from the cost price — by factor or with the
   full 5-step calculation including VAT.

2) Nothing gets lost
   Automatic backup before every save and restore with a review view — every
   price change stays under control.

3) Built for large catalogs
   Filters, batch saving, Excel import/export and autosave — fast and clear even
   with thousands of variants.
```
